About this simulator

The CPF Cry$tal Ball is a financial education simulator that models year-by-year retirement projections under official 2026 CPF rules. It is calibrated to the dollar against published FRS, ERS, BHS, and contribution rate values.

The simulator is a teaching tool, not financial advice. It models documented simplifications: no salary growth, no drawdown phase, no tax-savings modelling. Always verify with the CPF Board for your own circumstances.
