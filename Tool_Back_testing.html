1 · Lump sum: put it all in on day one

Same dollar amount into every counter on the common start date, then left alone until the common end date.

2 · Yearly investing: a fixed amount every January

The yearly amount goes in on the first trading day of each year after the start date. Total cash put in = yearly amount × number of years.

Growth per year here is "money-weighted": it allows for the fact that each year's top-up had a different length of time to grow.
